📌 Real-World Scenarios

🔴 Aggressive Timeline

Client demands product in 3 months instead of 6 months. What must we adjust?

↑ Cost (More resources needed)
↓ Scope (Reduce features)

💚 Budget Constraint

Budget is cut by 40% due to company cost-cutting measures. What happens?

↑ Time (Longer schedule)
↓ Scope (Fewer features)

⭐ Unlimited Scope

Client wants ALL features: mobile, web, admin panel, integrations, etc.

↑ Time (Much longer)
↑ Cost (Higher budget)

📊 SDLC Main Stages & Constraints
1️⃣ Planning & Requirements

CRITICAL Define scope, estimate time & cost. Set baseline constraints.

2️⃣ Analysis & Design

IMPORTANT Refine estimates. Identify resource needs. Update constraints based on findings.

3️⃣ Development

MONITOR Track actual time & cost. Monitor scope creep. Make trade-off decisions.

4️⃣ Testing

RISK Quality testing impacts timeline. May require schedule adjustments.

5️⃣ Deployment

EXECUTION Final validation of scope. Time-critical activities. Resource intensive.

6️⃣ Maintenance

ONGOING Continues support. Budget impacts. Scope may expand with enhancements.

🔄 Agile & Constraint Management
How Agile Handles Constraints:
Sprint-Based Timeline

Fixed sprint duration (usually 2 weeks). FIXED Helps predict schedule and costs.

Prioritized Scope

Features prioritized by value. FLEXIBLE Lower-priority features defer if needed.

Adaptive Budget

Iterative delivery. VARIABLE Stop when budget/timeline reached. Deliver MVP (Minimum Viable Product).

Continuous Feedback

Regular reviews with stakeholders. INFORMED Adjust constraints based on real progress.

Quality Focus

Testing in every sprint. MAINTAINED Quality never sacrificed; scope adjusted instead.

✨ Agile Advantage: By using sprints and prioritization, Agile allows flexible scope while keeping time and quality fixed. Perfect for uncertain requirements!
