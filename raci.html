RACI matrix

A RACI matrix is a grid that shows, for every task or deliverable, who does the work, who owns the result, who gives input, and who just needs to be kept informed. It stops two common problems: work that nobody owns, and decisions that everyone thinks someone else is making.

PMBOK term: RACI is the most common type of Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RAM). It's created in Plan Resource Management and becomes part of the resource management plan.

The four letters

Each cell in the matrix holds one letter for one person on one task.

R

Responsible

Does the work. A task can have several people responsible, but must have at least one.

e.g. the developer who writes the code.

A

Accountable

Owns the result and signs off. Exactly one person per task. The buck stops here.

e.g. the project manager approving the build.

C

Consulted

Gives input before the work is done. Two-way communication.

e.g. teachers asked what the grade screen needs.

I

Informed

Told about progress or the outcome after decisions are made. One-way communication.

e.g. the sponsor receiving the weekly status report.

How to build a RACI matrix

  1. List the work. Use deliverables or work packages from the WBS as rows.
  2. List the roles. Use roles (Business Analyst) rather than names, so the matrix survives people changing.
  3. Assign A first. Exactly one accountable person per row.
  4. Assign R. Who will actually do the work?
  5. Add C and I sparingly. Each C slows the work; each I adds a message to send.
  6. Review it with the team. Everyone should agree with their letters before work starts.

How to read the finished matrix

  • Read across a row to see who's involved in one task.
  • Read down a column to see one person's workload. A column full of R's means that person is overloaded.
  • A row with no A: nobody will make the final call.
  • A row with two A's: conflicting decisions and finger-pointing.
  • A column with no letters: does that person need to be on the project?

Variants you may see

  • RASCI: adds S for Support (helps the person who's responsible).
  • RACI-VS: adds Verifier and Signatory.
  • DACI: Driver, Approver, Contributor, Informed, used for decisions.

📝 PMP exam tips

  • "Only one person can be Accountable" is the rule most often tested.
  • RACI is a type of RAM. A RAM can also be organized by WBS work package and show only "who"; RACI adds the type of involvement.
  • Created during Plan Resource Management; documented in the resource management plan.
  • If a question describes confusion over who approves deliverables or team members doing duplicate work, the answer is often "review or create the RACI chart".
  • R and A can be the same person on small tasks. Some organizations write this as "A/R".
