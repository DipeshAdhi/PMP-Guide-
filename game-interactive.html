A school needed an online system to manage academic and administrative operations. Using Prototype Methodology, they built iterative prototypes with student feedback.

🛒 QuickShop E-Commerce
High-Pressure Launch
E-commerce startup needed to launch in 60 days to catch holiday season. Aggressive timeline required tight scope management.
Challenge: Tight deadline, limited team, full expectations
Solution: Agile sprints, prioritize must-haves, enhance later
🏥 MediCare Platform
Complex & Regulated
Healthcare provider built patient management system with strict compliance requirements. Quality was non-negotiable despite budget limits.
Challenge: Regulations, quality needs, limited budget
Solution: Extended timeline, reduced scope, invested in QA
